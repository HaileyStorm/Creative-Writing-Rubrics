# Sample scene (illustrative only)

Rain ticked on the bus shelter. Mara counted the coins again, then stopped, because counting would not make a fourth fare appear. The route board still promised the last inland run at 11:40. A man with a cello case stood too close to the timetable and did not look at it.

When the bus finally opened, the driver said her name as if it were a receipt. Mara did not ask how he knew it. She sat over the wheel well and watched the wet fields unspool, trying to decide whether arriving was the same thing as going back.
