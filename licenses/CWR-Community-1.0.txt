"Company Group" means a for-profit business and all entities it controls, that

of more than half of the voting rights or the power to direct management.
A passive minority investment alone does not create control.

"Annual Revenue" means consolidated worldwide gross revenue from all business
activities for the most recently completed fiscal year, before expenses, with
transactions within the Company Group eliminated to avoid double counting.
A business without a completed fiscal year uses its actual revenue since
formation; projections, capital raised, asset value, and profit are not revenue.
Non-USD revenue is converted using the fiscal period's average exchange rate
published by the relevant central bank or another consistently used reputable
financial source.

A "Large Company" is a Company Group with Annual Revenue of at least
US $10,000,000. The threshold is fixed in USD, without an employee-count test
or automatic inflation adjustment.
