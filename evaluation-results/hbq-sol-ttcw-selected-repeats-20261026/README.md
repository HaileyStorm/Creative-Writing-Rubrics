# Sol / TTCW: selected cycle-2 repeats

These are descriptive repeat comparisons from **two selected complete HBQ banks**, with scalar comparisons across **three stories overall**. The fixed plan selected 174 request identities; 118 were excluded by retained ancestry. All 56 eligible cycle-2 requests were attempted and accepted. Excluded ancestry does not fill this run or add observations.

Across 356 matched raw HBQ leaf states, **338 agreed (94.9438%)**, with Cohen’s kappa **0.913515**. The differences comprise 15 YES/NO flips and three applicability transitions; no CANNOT_ASSESS transition occurred. These leaves are clustered within two selected banks, not independent population replicates. Raw states are distinct from the scorer’s ladder-effective treatment.

HBQ uses its native 0–100 scale. Both scores decreased, with no reversal of their two-bank ordering. This does not establish systematic drift or a general ranking property.

All **seven available comparator scalar comparisons were unchanged**: two compact, two holistic, two Oregon and one TTCW14. Their native scales and retained values are in aggregates.json. Coarse or compressed scores can remain identical despite other response differences; unchanged values alone do not establish greater reliability or validity.

All **three available same-orientation pair returns were unchanged**. Of two pair groups, one has both assessed presentation orders and is directionally consistent; the other remains incomplete. One complete reverse-order pair cannot establish a causal order effect or general pair-judgment repeatability.

Exact saved evidence sets were identical for only **9/356 matched leaf pairs**. This measures exact evidence-text/set overlap, not semantic correctness, valid support or equivalence of reasoning. No response quotations are published here.

The analysis specification was frozen before the controller inspected these response values. Retained native evidence observes Sol/high and zero tool activity; it does not attest backend, auxiliary-path, full-wire, sampler or historical-account equivalence. Identity fencing was bounded to the retained initial view and named historical metadata; a complete historical failed-identity inventory is not claimed.

No confidence interval, population reliability, human-alignment gain, method superiority or candidate promotion is established. The saved analysis replayed 56 native receipts and made four canonical score-bundle calls, with zero bootstrap calls, new provider calls or human-target reads. This aggregate export only copies saved statistics and summarizes their counts; it performs no native replay, scoring, label read or provider call. The preceding collection’s 56 requests are not zero-contact evidence.
