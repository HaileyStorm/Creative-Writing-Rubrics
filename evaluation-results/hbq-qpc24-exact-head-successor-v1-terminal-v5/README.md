the required receipt and settings completion step. The public verifier checks
that declaration and its arithmetic; it does not independently inspect the
private receipt tree or establish the cause, provider outcome or model behavior.

13 contacted calls: 12 accepted voting calls covering 269 positions and one
structured but nonvoting call covering 24 positions. It declares the remaining
137 calls and 3,022 positions untouched. These are historical declarations, not
independently verified physical-contact counts, current capacity, or permission
to retry or resume. This package reports no discrimination or model-quality
result.

Only an opaque SHA-256 commitment to the private receipt tree is published.
No identifiers, content, quotations, locations, sessions, or per-leaf verdict
states are included here. The private receipt tree remains the reconstruction
authority. The published commitment alone does not identify its local location
or specify a reconstruction procedure; those remain separate evidence gaps.
