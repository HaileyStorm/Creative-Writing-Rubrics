# Child20: remaining TRAIN Grok expansion

Across the 44 frozen TRAIN items not used by the earlier four-item V11 screen,
the unchanged child20 prompt reduced equal-group mean item MAE from **1.230934
to 0.890993 (27.62%)**. Nineteen of 22 prompt groups improved; three worsened.

This is an 88-cell, 44-item, 22-group **development** measurement, not fresh
confirmation. It does not open DEV or confirmation items, establish a Sol
result, pool endpoints, select a prompt, promote a runtime, or establish
general literary or ranking success. The source panel keeps the previously
measured V11 TRAIN items and all earlier results separate.

Independent CSV and native-receipt replay found exact prompt-story joins, 88
distinct request IDs, 88 distinct session IDs, and all six numeric dimensions
for every cell. Every finite numeric score is retained under the frozen rule:
all 528 coverage flags were true, and no observed vector was all zero. Native
endpoint-contact cardinality remains unproven. Private source prose, payloads,
targets, item scores, native bodies, and identifiers are not published here.

For calibration context, a fixed non-fitted constant prediction of `3` has
MAE `0.776094`, lower than child20 by `0.114899`. This post-hoc diagnostic was
not a candidate, vote, tuning input, or preregistered comparator.

The post-hoc descriptive rank context is adverse: child20 is higher than
baseline in only two of six average-tie Spearman correlations across 44 items,
and lower in all six correlations after aggregation to 22 group means. These
stdlib-only calculations have no p-values and are not part of the primary MAE
comparison; the exact values are retained in `result.json`.

The requested judge was Grok `grok-4.6` at high reasoning; the recorded model
was `grok-4.6-build`, with tools and web disabled and no provider attestation.
The [aggregate result](result.json) and [public provenance commitments](provenance.json)
bind this publication to the frozen V13 study and independently replayed report.
