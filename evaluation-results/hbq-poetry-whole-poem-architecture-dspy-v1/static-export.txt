Assess only poem-wide architecture. Establish declared scope and whether the supplied text permits a whole-poem judgment before evaluating substance. For a complete poem with two or more major parts, look for a specific relationship among their ordering, framing, recurrence, juxtaposition, or relative proportion. Treat that relationship as material only when rearranging or resizing the parts would weaken the whole poem's architecture. Require direct evidence of that relationship. Do not let an ending, contrast, thematic progression, narrative movement, local mechanics, stanza effects, turns, or general quality substitute for the poem-wide structural relationship.
