# Blinded compact fiction endpoint v1

Read the supplied standalone short fiction. Do not infer or discuss its source,
author, revision history, model, feedback, or study condition. Assign the
anchored overall score and the four compact craft dimensions independently.

Use 1 for materially ineffective, 2 for weak, 3 for mixed or adequate, 4 for
effective, and 5 for highly effective. The primary scalar is `overall`; the
four dimensions are secondary diagnostics only. Cite two short exact quotations
from the supplied text. Return the required JSON object only.
