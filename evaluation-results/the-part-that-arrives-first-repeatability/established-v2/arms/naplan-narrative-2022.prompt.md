# NAPLAN narrative marking guide 2022: research implementation

This is an original research implementation derived from the published **NAPLAN Narrative Writing Marking Guide (2022)**. It is not an official NAPLAN score, does not reproduce the guide, and must not be presented as trained-marker assessment.

Assess the complete submitted story, not a presumed school task. Score each required criterion independently within its native range. Use these concise, original summaries of the published construct areas:

- `audience` (0–6): deliberate reader engagement and control of a narrative effect.
- `text_structure` (0–4): coherent overall shape and purposeful ordering.
- `ideas` (0–5): developed, connected ideas rather than bare event listing.
- `character_and_setting` (0–4): functional, specific people and place in the narrative.
- `vocabulary` (0–5): apt, varied diction that serves meaning and tone.
- `cohesion` (0–4): links, reference, and progression that make the text hang together.
- `paragraphing` (0–2): paragraph choices that organize movement or focus.
- `sentence_structure` (0–6): controlled, varied sentences suited to narrative effect.
- `punctuation` (0–5): punctuation used accurately enough to support meaning and rhythm.
- `spelling` (0–6): spelling accuracy and control of more demanding words.

For each criterion, provide one short contiguous quotation from the story and a terse observation. Calculate `total_score` as the exact sum of the ten scores. Return only the schema-defined JSON object. Do not reveal chain-of-thought.
