# What HBQ caught in *The Part That Arrives First*

These examples show the kinds of specific craft judgments HBQ can preserve. They were chosen after scoring as illustrations, not preregistered hypotheses. The [complete story](source.md) is published with the author's permission; the [batching study](README.md) reports how stable the judgments were.

## The viewpoint owns its metaphors

> Mica's old body did not contain Mica now. June understood that.
>
> Her wrist did not.

`craft.narrative.point_of_view_and_focalization.ownership` was `YES` in all ten HBQ runs. The judgments identified a mechanism, not just vivid prose: the mechanical, theatrical, and bodily metaphors plausibly come from June's craft-trained perception.

## The dialogue listens

> “You should have told me before you accepted.”
>
> “Yes.”
>
> “Would you have changed your answer?”
>
> “No.”

`craft.narrative.dialogue.listening` was `YES` in all ten HBQ runs. Each reply answers the pressure inside the preceding line, even when the honest answer worsens the conflict. That is more useful than a general claim that the dialogue is sharp or natural.

## The conflict stays bilateral

> “Mine when I move. Yours when I load your bones. Both where that distinction fails.”

`craft.narrative.theme_and_subtext.counterpoint` was `YES` in all ten HBQ runs. Mica's autonomy, June's grief and bodily exposure, Mara's boundaries, and the value of their shared work remain valid at the same time. The story earns credit for resisting a clean moral winner.

## The repetition is real, but arguable

`penalty.repetition.semantic` and `penalty.repetition.explanation` were `NO` in all ten HBQ runs: later scenes sometimes restate the autonomy-versus-continuity conflict after action and dialogue have already made it legible. All five holistic runs named the same limitation. The compact analytic arm split between 4 and 5 for narrative architecture because it agreed that the calibration scenes recur but disagreed about whether each recurrence changes enough to earn its length. That is a useful example of stable detection around a genuinely difficult rating.
