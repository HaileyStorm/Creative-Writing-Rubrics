# HANNA optimizer v3

V3 changes the candidate pack, model allocation, and promotion policy without forking the v2 analyzer. It pins v2's frozen 48/13/19 split, equal-prompt-group endpoint, and selection key. Two parent controls are the lexicographically first maximum-Hamming-distance pair across the four v1 factor fields. Three exact descendants—`absence-defect-split`, `construct-lock-antihalo`, and `realization-without-midpoint`—are byte-bound to queue result `7c6df77366ad4739b15a986c50b13431` (SHA-256 `bf2f171c…a8376`). The generator requested Grok 4.6/high through `grok_exec`, reported `grok-4.6-build`, supplied requested-only identity evidence, and did not attest reasoning effort. An internal Sol subagent reviewed the unchanged candidate bytes; that review is a handoff with no native provider receipt, contact claim, or transport attestation. Candidate task, instruction, profile, schema, prompt, and story commitments are byte-identical across every model schedule.

Grok 4.6 is the high-throughput development endpoint: all five candidates run on all 61 train/development items (305 cells), and Grok's equal-group development metrics freeze one selection preview before Sol metrics are admitted. This is an efficiency choice, not permission to fit a Grok-specific prompt. Cross-model generalization is governing: Sol validates the already-frozen Grok choice and can veto it, but cannot substitute a Sol-favored alternative.

The Sol schedule uses the lexicographically first item from every active prompt group for every candidate: 24 train groups plus seven development groups, 155 cells. Representatives are deterministically interleaved train/development before candidate order. On the seven equally weighted development anchors, the Grok choice is validation-eligible only when its Sol macro Spearman is at least the predeclared baseline control's and its Sol MAE is no greater. Failure yields no candidate substitution.

Mandatory execution is 460 calls versus v1's 732, saving 272 calls (37.16%). Optional DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731/max and Luna/xhigh schedules each add 35 development anchors; both together bring the maximum to 530, still 202 below 732. Their exact provider/model identities are explicit, but their signals are provisional, nonblocking, and have no selection authority. Absence or failure cannot change the Grok/Sol gate.

DSPy and Optuna may be used only during development to optimize the Grok objective subject to the frozen Sol generalization gate. Neither is a runtime dependency or selection authority. Confirmation remains unopened.

V3 deliberately adds no receipt framework. Native execution must retain request hash, exact response-byte hash, provider, model, transport identity, and native contact identity, then project exact cells through the pinned v2 equal-group analyzer. Each preview decision binds the declared Grok and Sol metric inputs, exact scheduled-cell commitments, and current v3 implementation/contract hashes; those commitments do not turn caller-supplied aggregates into provider evidence. Until native trust is independently verified, schedules, metrics, selection commitments, and validation decisions remain nonempirical previews.
