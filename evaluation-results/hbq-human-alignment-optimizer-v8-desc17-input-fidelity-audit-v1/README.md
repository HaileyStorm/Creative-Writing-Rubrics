# Desc17 input-fidelity audit

This provider-free audit preserves the completed desc17 collector and its
published result as immutable history, but withdraws their semantic use. Every
one of the 52 admitted payloads carries only `prompt:<id>` and `writing:<id>`
rather than the prompt and writing needed for HANNA judgment. The same pinned
collector also contains ten all-zero response vectors, two `"x"` evidence
responses, and four placeholder or `Searching workspace` responses.

The audit therefore withdraws desc17 semantic and generalization conclusions.
It does not reinterpret or affect the separate desc15, desc16, or Fresh88
evidence. It makes no provider call and does not alter the original result.
