## Denominators and scope
The primary cohort is the exact all-five shared28; the secondary is the distinct exact HBQ numeric29. Both represent12 plots out of the planned36 works. HBQ expert correlations are copied from saved401 (0.7119170412541377 and0.6862598695958416), never recomputed. Other methods reuse exact-membership cached points when available; only newly needed same-cohort points are calculated. TTCW14 lacks one score on the fixed29 cohort and remains unavailable there, rather than silently switching to28. A constant-zero or constant native-score rank correlation is undefined, not zero.

Predetermined controls are constant0, Unicode lexical token count, and raw casefolded type/token ratio. Tokenization uses `[^\W_]+(?:['’][^\W_]+)*`: digits count, and underscores/hyphens separate tokens. Types use casefold only, without stemming, Unicode normalization, stopword removal or fitted weights. This count is not a linguistic word count. Raw type/token ratio inherently depends on length. Directions remain raw; no transformation or post-target selection occurs.

Each control/expert association and HBQ/control association is newly calculated. HBQ-minus-control is a point difference on exactly the same works. This is a cheap objection check, not residualized or causal modeling: both strong and weak feature associations admit several explanations, including plot/condition dependence and actual aspects of writing. It cannot distinguish quality from verbosity or establish a method winner, improvement or human-alignment gain.

The original33-shared-work rule is unmet; availability selection and within12-plot dependence remain. No bootstrap or new confidence interval is estimated. Universal full36 missing-outcome bounds[-1,1] for correlations and[-2,2] for differences are not confidence intervals or tight identified bounds. Existing expert means are42 binary ballots per story, not42 independent experts or direct preference votes. No unused confirmation or new labels are opened.
