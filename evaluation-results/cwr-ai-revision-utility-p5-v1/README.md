# Prospective AI revision utility: development outputs and blind evaluation freeze

Three fresh native OpenAI Sol/high conversations generated 18 distinct first drafts: six short stories, six bounded novel excerpts and six free-verse poems. All passed the frozen mechanical schema, identity, word/line/context bounds and exact preservation-anchor checks. This does not establish creative quality or revision utility. Full native journals and verbatim finals are retained locally.

The protocol assigns 12 roots to development and six to held-back confirmation before generation. Development texts are in `originals-development.json`; held-back prose stays local. Their generators and custody controller can see their sources, so source invisibility is not claimed. Preservation anchors are generator proposals, not literary or human labels.

The development stage now retains 24 feedbacks and 24 revisions, one per feedback arm for each development root. Six fresh Sol/high conversations produced each stage on the same primary native account as the original generators. Every feedback contains exactly 180 words and at most three source-quoted issues. CWR feedback received the complete correctly scoped bank; generic feedback received no rubric. Editors received only the original, shared creative context and feedback body, with explicit arm labels and rubric metadata excluded. All 24 revisions passed the frozen schema, identity, word/line and unchanged-context checks. These checks establish no creative quality, voice preservation or utility.

Twenty revisions have admitted native execution. The generic excerpt batch `p5-rv-04` is retained with an execution exception: its first read supplied the complete packet but used a nonexistent schema path. The controller recorded the exact frozen schema for inline recovery in the same conversation. Two coordination messages and a wait exceeded the frozen arithmetic-only additional-tool scope; encrypted native coordination records do not prove plaintext schema delivery. Three existing drafts were byte-exact in the final response, but `r08` changed one paragraph, increasing its count from 453 to 456 words. No fresh editor or replacement final was generated. The original failed receipt and full journals remain local. `revisions-summary.json` records this exception and the separate full-development and clean-execution comparison sets before any evaluation results exist. Recovered excerpt results cannot alone establish utility under clean matched execution.

One feedback retention check was reconciled against the frozen arithmetic-only contract: an empty local word-count test label was permitted, while all four final root IDs and feedbacks were correct. The original failure remains retained; no model call was repeated.

The arms are unchanged original, strong generic-feedback revision and CWR-feedback revision. Feedback length, issue cap, creative context, revision word allowance and native model/account/effort were matched by the frozen packets. The recovered batch prevents a claim of identical execution. Equal hidden reasoning compute or sampler budgets also cannot be proved. Blind Grok/secondary-Sol evaluation has not run; its exact development requests are now frozen, and held-back prose remains sealed.

`prepare_evaluation.py` freezes 2,592 requests, 1,296 per endpoint: 1,026 complete-bank packets, 54 holistic, 54 compact, 54 source-comparison voice/introduced-defect and 108 pairwise requests. The three scoped banks retain 178 story, 170 excerpt and 89 poetry leaves, packed in groups of at most eight. All three interventions receive three cycles on the registered roots `r01`, `r07` and `r13`; every within-root pair is evaluated in both orders. Judges receive opaque artifact IDs, unchanged title/brief/work context, and correctly scoped task context. Feedback and proposed preservation-anchor metadata are excluded from judge prompts. The craft comparators are named scope adaptations; poetry judgments respect contemplative and circular forms without imposing narrative, meter or rhyme. These are not established poetry instruments or demonstrated human alignment.

`evaluation-design.json` fixes root-clustered analysis, per-endpoint/form results, ties at half, 10,000 bootstrap resamples with seed 20261004, order/missingness reporting, and separate full-development and clean-execution sensitivity. Intended feedback criteria are retained only for post-judgment joins. Source-grounded voice and introduced-defect judgments remain proposals. `validate_evaluation.py` checks frozen schemas, source quotation grounding, complete criterion/dimension coverage, evidence counts and abstention consistency. Offline preflight passed 108 synthetic positive/negative cases; readback verified all 1,148 frozen artifacts and every request/source/context/schema commitment. `evaluation-freeze-summary.json` records these preparation results. They do not establish native provider acceptance or revision utility.

`collector.py` now connects the frozen P5 inputs to the retained native collector, complete-schema admission and saved-attempt replay. It preserves Sol native receipts, Grok v5 one-turn/no-tool attestations, invocation/account identity, one attempt per logical sample, STOP handling and settlement of in-flight calls. Both collection and replay reconstruct the same source and task/work context. A local composed check exercised all five instruments on all three forms: 30 synthetic accepted/rejected collection-and-replay cases, each rejecting a changed saved source. It used synthetic native doubles and establishes no provider acceptance. One static review found and corrected a 900-versus-960-second route-margin mismatch; the 960-second boundary has a local regression check.

Execution still requires fresh secondary-account identity, an eligible exact route, disk space and owner-verified global headroom. The disk gate remains 1 GiB for a new launch and 256 MiB before contact, with at most ten simultaneous requests per endpoint and no ambiguous resend. Grok uses native reasoning defaults; the retained route's `high` request-metadata field is not an applied or attested reasoning selector. Outbound requests contain only selected development AI prose, neutral contexts, prompts and schemas. The development gate replays both complete endpoint denominators before a separate recorded controller decision and unchanged-mechanism freeze can release held-back roots. No P5 evaluation has contacted a provider.

`prepare.py`, `prepare_feedback.py`, `prepare_revisions.py` and `prepare_evaluation.py` freeze each stage from pinned sources. The corresponding `validate_*` files perform mechanical checks. Earlier protocol and stage summaries remain immutable snapshots; their prospective flags describe those earlier stages. Later development files and summaries record their outputs, hashes, actual settings, admission and execution exception. Full source journals, exact prompts/schemas and projection recipes remain locally retained. This package creates no human labels, promotes no rubric candidate and does not complete the full program.
