# Prospective AI revision utility: original generation

Three fresh native OpenAI Sol/high conversations generated 18 distinct first drafts: six short stories, six bounded novel excerpts and six free-verse poems. All passed the frozen mechanical schema, identity, word/line/context bounds and exact preservation-anchor checks. This does not establish creative quality or revision utility. Full native journals and verbatim finals are retained locally.

The protocol assigns 12 roots to development and six to held-back confirmation before generation. Development texts are in `originals-development.json`; held-back prose stays local. Their generators and custody controller can see their sources, so source invisibility is not claimed. Preservation anchors are generator proposals, not literary or human labels.

The planned arms are unchanged original, strong generic-feedback revision and CWR-feedback revision. Feedback length, issue cap, source context, revision word allowance and native model/account/effort are matched. CWR's complete correctly scoped question bank is the intervention. Equal hidden reasoning compute or sampler budgets cannot be proved through this native surface. Feedback, revisions and evaluation have not yet run; exact evaluation requests are not yet frozen.

`prepare.py` creates the prospective protocol and generation packets from the pinned matched campaign's canonical banks (story178, excerpt170, poetry89). `validate_originals.py` performs mechanical admission. `protocol.json` and `originals-summary.json` preserve the frozen design, root hashes, partition, actual settings and retained provenance. This package does not relabel historical revision studies, create human labels, promote a rubric candidate or complete the full program.
