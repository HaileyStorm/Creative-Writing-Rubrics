# Short scene-boundary review

Each numbered case contains an ending and the passage immediately following it.
Assess these questions separately, using only the supplied text:

1. Does the ending avoid substituting arbitrary cliffhangers or withheld basics
   for earned momentum?
2. Does the exit place emphasis effectively and create an appropriate relation
   to what follows?

For each question give a provisional YES, NO, NOT_APPLICABLE, or CANNOT_ASSESS;
include one short exact quote, your reason, and a plausible alternative reading.
Then assess whether these examples can distinguish the questions and identify
confounds. These are synthetic texts; your review is not a human rating.

## Case 01

Scene: Inez unfolded the inspector's note beside the pump. The water had risen
to the third stair, and the night crew was waiting for her decision. She read
the single instruction twice, then sent the crew toward the locked shed. By the
time the shed door yielded, the pumps had stopped.

Following passage: The note had told her to leave the pumps running. Inez
watched the crew return through the flooded yard and began explaining why she
had sent them away from the switches.

## Case 02

Scene: Inez unfolded the inspector's note beside the pump. The water had risen
to the third stair, and the night crew was waiting for her decision. The note
said to leave the pumps running. She sent the crew to the locked shed for the
spare cable; by the time the door yielded, the pumps had stopped.

Following passage: Inez watched the crew return through the flooded yard and
began explaining why she had sent them away from the switches. A severed cable
lay across the wet steps.

## Case 03

Scene: At the hearing, Daro placed the repaired watch on the table. His sister
had asked him to admit he broke it, and he had rehearsed the admission all the
way there. He told the panel the truth. The chair thanked him and called a recess.

Following passage: On the landing, his sister held out her hand. Daro returned
the watch, and she wound it once, listening for the uneven tick they both knew.

## Case 04

Scene: At the hearing, Daro placed the repaired watch on the table. His sister
had asked him to admit he broke it, and he had rehearsed the admission all the
way there. He told the panel the truth. The chair thanked him and called a recess.

Following passage: On the landing, a clerk announced that the building's west
stairs would be repainted on Tuesday. Daro read the notice twice. His sister
waited beside the door without speaking.
