# CWR licensing

CWR's newly licensed application content uses the
[CWR Community License 1.0](../licenses/CWR-Community-1.0.txt).
HBQ-RS itself remains [Apache 2.0](../licenses/Apache-2.0.txt): its standard,
engine, schemas, implementation examples, and generic judge protocols.
The root [LICENSE](../LICENSE) gives the authoritative file scope.

| User or activity | Permission under the community license |
| --- | --- |
| Individual acting personally; nonprofit, education, or public body | Free, including commercial use where applicable |
| Company group below US $10 million annual revenue | Free commercial use, modification, services, and resale |
| Company group at or above US $10 million annual revenue | Separate negotiated permission for restricted CWR material |
| Small provider delivering independent finished assessments to any client | Free; client instructions or submission of an artifact alone do not trigger enterprise licensing |
| Large company using a self-service CWR product or API | Separate negotiated permission |

Company size uses **consolidated worldwide annual gross revenue**, with common
control and subsidiaries counted together and internal transactions eliminated.
It uses the most recently completed fiscal year, or actual revenue since
formation for a new business. Revenue means neither ARR nor profit, investment,
or valuation. Passive minority investment alone does not combine companies.
A 90-day transition covers crossing the threshold or acquisition; the license
text defines its exact limits.

Smaller users may sell copies or derivatives and keep their own additions
proprietary. Protected CWR material retains its license and notices downstream;
a reseller cannot grant an enterprise broader rights to that material.
There is no mandatory source publication, royalty, financial reporting, or
blanket ban on resale. Permission to upstream CWR does not license additions
owned by someone else.

Independent software, ordinary creative outputs, ideas, and scoring methods
do not acquire this condition merely through association with CWR.
Third-party sources and dependencies retain their own terms. For separately
negotiated permissions, contact Hailey Collet, identified in [NOTICE](../NOTICE),
through the [repository owner's profile](https://github.com/HaileyStorm).

Earlier Apache grants remain valid, including the material available through
commit `8cda421aae3bfbb82a5ca3c4a3d3a5a553f534e8` and earlier releases.
The new restriction chiefly protects future original expression first offered
under it; changing LICENSE cannot withdraw the permissions already granted.
The released 1.2.3 citation, frozen study inputs, and historical research
packages retain their existing licensing and provenance.
[Apache 2.0, section 2](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0.html)
states the earlier grant's irrevocable terms.

The company-size condition makes CWR content **source available**, rather than
OSI open source. HBQ-RS remains Apache open source.
See the [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd).
Copyright protects eligible expression, not ideas or methods; derivative
copyright covers contributed material rather than expanding rights to the
preexisting work. See [17 USC 102–103](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html).
