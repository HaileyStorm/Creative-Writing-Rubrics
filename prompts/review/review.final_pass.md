# Restrained final-pass review

Follow `prompts/review/_shared.md`.

Look only for remaining clarity, continuity, awkwardness, excess, and mechanical problems. Do not intensify everything, overwrite voice, or beautify every sentence. If a proposed fix would add facts or rewrite a protected span, reject it.
