# Long-form evaluation protocol

Do not read a long manuscript as an undifferentiated block and do not average chapter grades.

Required passes:
1. Build a whole-work map: units, chronology, POVs, central arcs, subplots, promises, motifs, and ending state.
2. Build state ledgers for characters, knowledge, relationships, injuries, objects, locations, and world-rule changes.
3. Identify load-bearing units and distant setup/payoff pairs.
4. Select stratified local samples: opening, ending, major turns, quiet units, action units, exposition-heavy units, dialogue-heavy units, and suspected weak regions.
5. Apply local questions only to evidence-bearing units.
6. Apply global questions to the whole map and ledgers.
7. Test retrieval of distant facts and superseding updates.
8. Report systemic patterns separately from isolated defects.
9. Report evidence coverage, context omissions, conflicts, and cannot-assess questions.
10. Escalate when the evidence packet exceeds reliable context or score intervals remain materially wide.
