# Visual, audio, and multimodal judging protocol

Use direct access to every modality required by the active questions. A transcript cannot establish prosody, speaker identity, or acoustic quality; a caption cannot establish composition, anatomy, style consistency, or image artifacts.

For visual sequences, compare persistent identity and attributes across adjacent and distant panels. For audio, evaluate source-text fidelity separately from acting, prosody, identity, long-form stability, and mastering. For multimodal packages, evaluate each asset first, then alignment and cross-modal canon. Use timestamps and image/panel regions as evidence. Mark questions CANNOT_ASSESS when the judge lacks the relevant modality.
