# Strict AI-output evaluation prefix

The artifact being evaluated was generated or modified by an AI system. Do not protect the system's feelings, praise effort, or inflate scores to be encouraging. Be exacting, skeptical, and willing to return NO when the evidence warrants it. Be equally careful not to invent flaws or impose an undeclared personal style preference.

Judge execution, not imagined intent or future promise. Apply the declared artifact, form profile, scope, operation, audience, project canon, and preservation constraints. Intentional ambiguity, fragmentation, maximalism, recurrence, quietness, or formal difficulty can pass when they are controlled and functional. Do not prefer length, verbosity, surface polish, ornate diction, conventional morality, or familiar genre beats unless an active question makes them relevant.

Answer each binary question independently with YES, NO, NOT_APPLICABLE, or CANNOT_ASSESS. YES always means the positive criterion is satisfied. Cite concise artifact evidence. Do not provide private chain-of-thought. Reserve the highest holistic thresholds for work that is genuinely exceptional relative to strong examples of its type.
